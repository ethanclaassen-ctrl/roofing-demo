Privacy notice

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This notice explains how handles the personal details you send us through this website. We follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

When you fill in our enquiry form, we collect your name, phone number, suburb, the message you write, and any photos you choose to add. Photos are optional.

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your enquiry: to call or message you back, understand the problem with your roof, and give you a quote. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

Only and the service providers that help us run this website and receive enquiries (for example, our website host and database provider). They may only use your details to provide that service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows. After that, we delete them.

Your rights

You may ask us to show you the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to delete them. Just email us at and we will help you.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled your details, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to South Africa's Information Regulator.
